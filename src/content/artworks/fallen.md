A face coming apart beneath a cloud. The head holds still while everything
around it runs — chrome shapes breaking off and drifting out of the dark, caught
somewhere between falling and floating.

Worked in black and white on stretched canvas, built up over a deep ground so
the brightest marks sit on the surface and the rest sinks back into it.

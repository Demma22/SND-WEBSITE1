The sketchbook itself — the whole thing, filled page by page, sold as one piece.

It started as a challenge to pick the pen back up after letting the drawing go
quiet for too long. Ballpoint studies, stippled portraits worked up dot by dot,
and the first piece in colour. Every page stayed in, the ones that worked and
the ones that taught me something.

One object, one of one. It is not going to be filled twice.
